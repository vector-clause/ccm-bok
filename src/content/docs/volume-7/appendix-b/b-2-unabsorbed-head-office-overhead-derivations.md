## Scope as outlined

Mathematical Formulations for Eichleay, Hudson, and Emden Equations

### Hudson

$$
\text{Claim} = \left(\frac{HO\% + P\%}{100}\right) \times \frac{\text{Contract Sum}}{\text{Contract Period}} \times \text{Period of Delay}
$$

### Emden

$$
\text{Claim} = \left(\frac{\text{Actual Total Enterprise Overhead \& Profit \%}}{100}\right) \times \frac{\text{Contract Sum}}{\text{Contract Period}} \times \text{Period of Delay}
$$

### Eichleay

$$
\text{Allocable Overhead} = \frac{\text{Project Billings}}{\text{Total Billings}} \times \text{Total Overhead}
$$

$$
\text{Daily Overhead Rate} = \frac{\text{Allocable Overhead}}{\text{Days of Performance}}
$$

$$
\text{Claim} = \text{Daily Overhead Rate} \times \text{Days of Delay}
$$
